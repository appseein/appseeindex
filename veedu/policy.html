Veedu - All India Construction is an app by Appsee. We use AdMob for advertisements and Firebase for saving and supporting app-related data.

We collect information that you provide directly to us when using the Veedu platform, including personal details necessary for construction services and localization preferences.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to facilitate connections between users, laborers, and material suppliers across India, ensuring a seamless construction process.

We use the information to:

Your selected language preference is stored to provide a localized experience throughout the Veedu application.
