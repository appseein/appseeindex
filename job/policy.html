Privacy Policy

Job Bazaar by Appsee

Effective Date: July 2026

1. No Personal Data

We respect your privacy. Job Bazaar does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal details. You are not required to create an account, register, or provide an email address to use this application.

2. Resume Uploads (ATS)

Our AI Resume ATS feature allows you to upload a document to match with jobs. This process happens entirely locally on your device. We do not upload, save, or share your resume files with our servers or any third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

To keep this app free and functional, we use trusted third-party services that may collect specific non-personal data:

  • Firebase Used securely for hosting our job database and providing real-time feed updates.
  • Google AdMob Used to display advertisements. AdMob may use advertising IDs and cookies to serve personalized ads based on your interests.

4. External Job Links

All job postings inside Job Bazaar are manually curated and collected from official company career pages by the Appsee team. Clicking "Apply Now" will redirect you to these external, official websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third-party sites once you leave our app.
