Privacy Policy

Last Updated:

1. Your Data Stays on Your Device

We believe in keeping things simple and private. Daily Fortune Buzzer does NOT collect, store, or transmit your personal data to any external servers. Everything you do in the app—including your stars (points), daily buzzer presses, and generated fortunes—is saved locally right on your device.

2. Google AdMob Advertising

To keep this app free, we use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob is a third-party service provided by Google.

  • AdMob may collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, and usage data to provide relevant ads.
  • This data collection is managed entirely by Google according to their privacy guidelines.
  • For more information on how Google uses this data, please read the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Entertainment & "Guess Only" Disclaimer

Daily Fortune Buzzer is an entertainment app. The fortunes, predictions, and messages provided by the buzzers are generated randomly using mathematical algorithms ("guess only"). They are meant strictly for fun and do not reflect real-life predictions, advice, or actual fortune-telling.

4. Permissions

Because the app runs in your browser/device and data is saved locally, we do not require invasive permissions like camera, microphone, or contacts.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if we add new features. We advise you to review this page periodically for any changes.
