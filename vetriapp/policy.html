Privacy Policy for Kumbakonam Vetri App

Effective Date: September 5, 2026 | Last Updated: September 5, 2026

Welcome to the Kumbakonam Vetri App. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy governs your use of the Kumbakonam Vetri App mobile application and official constituency portal developed by appsee.in.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the application, express an interest in obtaining constituency services, lodge a grievance/complaint, participate in public constituency polls, or otherwise contact us.

  • Personal Identification Information: Full name, email address, mobile phone number, and residential address.
  • Constituency Data: Complaints, grievances, and service requests you submit regarding local infrastructure.
  • Interactive Activity Data: Your votes on public constituency polls and feedback notes.
  • Authentication Data: Encrypted credentials used via Firebase Authentication to secure your account.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information collected via our app for various constituency management purposes described below:

  • Account Creation and Authentication: To manage user accounts, verify identity, and secure access using Firebase.
  • Grievance Resolution: To process, track, and route local civic complaints to the respective MLA office.
  • Constituency Polls & Feedback: To record public votes and display aggregated poll results.
  • Communication: To send updates regarding the status of your petitions and important announcements.

3. Sharing Your Information & Data Security

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to third parties. We use secure backend database storage and hosting services. You have direct control over your submitted complaints within a 3-hour window after submission, allowing you to edit or delete your data directly.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application is intended for citizens of the Kumbakonam constituency. We do not knowingly solicit data from or market to children under the age of 13.
