Privacy Policy

ShubhDinam · Last updated: 25 November 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the ShubhDinam app ("we", "our", "the app") collects, uses and shares information when you use it. By using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

Information you enter: When you create or view an event invitation (such as marriage, engagement, baby shower, housewarming, shop opening, ear-piercing, puberty function, land pooja, birthday or anniversary events), you may enter details such as names, event date and time, venue, a message, and a phone number.

Information stored on your device: Your language preference and the IDs of invitations you created are saved locally on your device so the app can remember them.

Automatically collected information: Our advertising partner (Google AdMob) may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system and app usage information to show and measure ads.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or payment information.

3. How We Use Information

  • To create, store and display the invitations you make.
  • To let people open an invitation through its link.
  • To remember your language and your saved invitations.
  • To show advertisements that support the app.
  • To maintain, secure and improve the app.

4. Firebase (Data Storage)

We use Google Firebase to store invitation data. Details you enter into an invitation are saved on Firebase servers so that the invitation can be opened and shared. Anyone who has an invitation's link can view the content of that invitation, so please do not enter information you do not want to share with the people you send the link to.

Firebase's handling of data is described in the Firebase privacy information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use the advertising ID and similar identifiers to show personalised or non-personalised ads, limit how often an ad is shown, and measure ad performance. You can learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers needed to run the app (Google Firebase and Google AdMob), when required by law, or when you share an invitation link yourself.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Invitation data is kept for as long as it is needed to provide the app. You can request deletion of your invitation data at any time by emailing us at the address below with the invitation link or details. We will remove it within a reasonable time. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes the information stored locally on your device.

8. Security

We use the security features provided by Firebase to protect stored data. However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Third-Party Links

The app or its ads may link to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices, and we encourage you to read their policies.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
