Privacy Policy

Cutlyra: Video Editor & Maker

Developer: AppSee  |  Effective date: 25 October 2026  |  Last updated: 25 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AppSee ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you use the Cutlyra: Video Editor & Maker mobile app ("the App"). We respect your privacy and we want you to understand clearly what the App does and does not do.

In short: Cutlyra edits your videos and photos on your own device. You do not need to create an account. We do not upload your videos, photos or music to our servers, and we do not sell your personal information.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not ask you to register or sign in. The App does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location or payment details.

2. Your Videos, Photos And Music

  • When you choose a video, photo or music file, the App reads that file only so you can edit it.
  • Editing and exporting happen on your device. Your files are not sent to us.
  • Your project settings and the files you are working on are kept in the App's storage on your device so you can continue later. You can clear them by removing the App or clearing its data in your phone settings.
  • The videos you export are saved by you to your own device. You decide where to keep them and who to share them with.

3. Permissions

The App asks for access only to the files you select, so that you can add them to your project and save your finished video. We use this access only for the editing features you use. We do not use it for any other purpose.

4. Advertising

The App may show ads provided by Google AdMob. Ad partners may collect and use certain information, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address and general device and app information, to show ads and to measure and improve ads. This information is handled by the ad provider under its own policy. You can learn more at policies.google.com/privacy and manage ad personalisation in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

5. Fonts And Third-Party Services

To show text styles, the App may load fonts from Google Fonts. When fonts are loaded from the internet, Google may receive technical information such as your IP address. Other than ad services and font loading, the App does not share your information with third parties.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share information only with the service providers described in this policy, or when required by law.

7. Data Security

Because your media stays on your device, it stays under your control. We take reasonable steps to keep the App safe, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Data Retention And Deletion

We do not store your personal data on our servers, so there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf. To remove the data the App keeps on your device, clear the App's storage or uninstall the App.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help.

10. Your Choices

  • You can control which files the App can access in your device settings.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings.
  • You can uninstall the App at any time.

11. Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We recommend that you review this page from time to time.
