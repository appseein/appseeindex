Bigil

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026 · App: Bigil (com.appsee.browser.bigil) · Developer: Appsee

In short

Bigil does not collect, sell or share your personal data. We have no accounts, no servers that receive your browsing data, and no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services. Your history, bookmarks and settings stay on your device.

1. What Bigil stores on your device

  • Browsing history – page titles, addresses and visit times, so you can find pages again.
  • Bookmarks – pages you save.
  • Download list – file names, source addresses and times. The files themselves are saved in your device Downloads folder.
  • Settings – for example your default search engine.

This information is kept only in Bigil’s private app storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to any third party by Bigil. Websites you visit may store cookies and site data in the browser, as in any browser.

2. Incognito mode

In incognito tabs, Bigil does not save history, the download list, form data or cache. Cookies and site data from the incognito session are deleted when you close all incognito tabs. Screenshots and the recent-apps preview are blocked while incognito is shown. Incognito does not hide your activity from the websites you visit, your network or your internet provider.

3. Websites and search engines

When you search or open a website, your device connects directly to that service, which receives normal technical information such as your IP address and browser details. Bigil lets you choose Brave Search, Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, Ecosia or Yahoo. Each has its own privacy policy, and we do not control how they handle data. Bigil does not add identifiers or tracking to your requests.

4. Permissions

  • Internet – to load web pages.
  • Notifications – to show media controls (play, pause, stop) while audio or video plays, and a notice while incognito tabs are open. You can decline this permission.
  • Foreground service (media playback) – keeps audio and video playing in the background with controls on the lock screen.
  • Downloads – files you choose to download are saved to your Downloads folder using Android’s download manager.
  • File upload – when a website asks for a file, Bigil opens the Android file picker. Only the file you select is shared with that website.

Bigil does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or SMS.

5. Third-party components

Bigil displays web pages using the Android System WebView provided by your device. Bigil contains no advertising SDKs and no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

6. Children

Bigil is a general-purpose browser and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

7. Your choices and deleting data

  • Delete history by time range (last hour, 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days or all time) on the History page.
  • Clear history, bookmarks and the download list from the Home page.
  • Close all incognito tabs to erase the incognito session.
  • Uninstalling Bigil removes all data it stores on your device. Downloaded files stay in your Downloads folder until you delete them.

8. Security

Bigil’s own pages run inside the app, and its storage is private to the app. No method of storing or transmitting data is perfectly secure, but because Bigil keeps your data on your device, we do not hold it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
